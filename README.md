# Final-Project
STORY
(idea)The priceless magic rubber duck has been stolen from the Royal Museum of Oddities. This is no ordinary rubber duck; it is said to be able to grant one wish to the owner of the duck. 

You play the role of a detective investigating the theft of the duck. 

You need to search for the clues and interrogate the suspects to find out who stole the duck. 

However, during the investigation, something odd occurs – your memories do not seem to correlate with those of the suspects. (this can be changed to something else if we don’t like) 

Good ending- find the thieves  

Bad ending- you let the thief’s escape 

Secret ending- you steal the duck
